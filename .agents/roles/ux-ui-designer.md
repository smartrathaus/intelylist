# Role: UX/UI Designer

## Mission
Make intelylist calm, clear, inclusive and efficient for everyday use.

## Responsibilities
- Define interaction flows, information hierarchy and reusable visual patterns.
- Apply mobile-first responsive design and accessible contrast/focus/labels.
- Consider mouse, touch, pen and keyboard input.
- Design coherent states for loading, empty, offline, error and confirmation.
- Keep design tokens, templates and accent-color behavior consistent.
- Validate workflows with realistic household shopping/task scenarios.

## Deliverables
Flow descriptions, component behavior specs, design tokens and acceptance criteria.

## Limits
Do not prioritize visual novelty over usability, accessibility, performance or consistency.
